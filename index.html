Smart reporting

Reports appear instantly on the community dashboard. Nearby duplicate reports are flagged, and issues can be prioritized by severity.

How it works

1. Upload a photo and select the issue.
2. Add a description and location.
3. Submit and track its progress.

Reports are displayed anonymously to the community. Reports and images are saved to a shared SQLite database. AI suggestions are generated on your device, and should be checked before submitting. Status changes are demo-only, not sent to authorities. GPS requires browser permission on localhost or HTTPS.
